Contact & FAQ

Common questions

How do you check battery fit?

Fitment is confirmed using the exact vehicle details and a photo of the existing battery label and terminal layout. Vehicle origin alone is not enough.

What should I send in a message?

Send the make, model, year, engine size if known, and photos of the battery label and terminals.

How do I confirm stock?

Contact the shop before travelling. Stock and availability must be confirmed directly with us.

What warranty applies?

The Atlas 45A has a 12-month warranty. Warranty details for other products must be confirmed before purchase.

Can I collect or arrange delivery?

Ask for pickup arrangements or delivery availability, service area, fees, and timing directly with the shop.
